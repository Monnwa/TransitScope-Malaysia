# Usability test plan

## Objectives and participants

Evaluate whether representative commuters and students can complete the three fixed tasks efficiently, understand simulated data status, and report satisfaction. Recruit at least five adults who use public transport or cycle in Klang Valley where feasible. Do not treat assumptions as findings.

## Environment and privacy

Use the deployed site or local laptop build, desktop and participant-owned mobile devices where permitted. Record only anonymous ID, device, task performance, timing, errors, observations and voluntary comments. Obtain consent; do not record names, contact details or screen video unless separately consented.

## Tasks and success criteria

| Task | Expected outcome | Success criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Locate a bus | Search `DEMO-BUS-101` or `D100`, select it, state location and freshness. | Correct record within 60 seconds. |
| B Filter transport services | Select Shared bike and verify non-bike modes leave map and list. | Correct filtering within 45 seconds. |
| C Investigate alert | Open the displayed alert and state severity, meaning, affected service and timestamp. | Correct response within 60 seconds. |

## Measurement procedure

Start timing when a facilitator reads the task. Stop at the stated correct outcome or time limit. Define an error as a wrong mode selection, irrelevant result selection, unsupported backtracking that requires facilitator recovery, or an incorrect final answer. Calculate completion rate as successful attempts / total attempts x 100. Calculate mean completion time only for successful attempts; report failed times separately. Calculate error rate as errors / task attempts. After all tasks, administer the standard ten-question SUS on a 1 strongly disagree to 5 strongly agree scale. Score odd items as response minus 1, even items as 5 minus response, sum, then multiply by 2.5.
